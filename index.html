Reminders (ntfy)

Install the ntfy app on your phone and subscribe to the topic below. Saving queues the next 3 days of reminders on the ntfy server, so open this app at least every 3 days to top them up.

Changing the times does not cancel reminders already queued, so you may get a few doubles for up to 3 days.

Patch-test any new product on your jawline for 24 hours before using it on your face. This is general guidance, not medical advice.
